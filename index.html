About

I build machine learning methods that make sense of cancer one cell at a time. My work combines graph representation learning, copy-number analysis, and spatial transcriptomics to identify tumor cells, trace how tumors evolve, and test what biological foundation models actually learn.

I am a Graduate Research Assistant in Prof. Xian Mallory's DeepBioMed Lab at Florida State University, working closely with the Maizie Zhou Lab at Vanderbilt University. I'm open to research internships and collaborations in machine learning for biology and health.

Research

Probing single-cell foundation models for cancer genome identity

Ongoing

Do transcriptomic foundation models such as scGPT encode which tumor clone a cell belongs to, or its copy-number state? I'm testing this by probing and adapting model embeddings against biologically defined ground truth.

DeepMalignant Under revision, Genome Research

Lead author

An unsupervised graph attention autoencoder that identifies tumor cells without labels by learning joint representations of scRNA-seq expression and copy number alteration (CNA) signals.

  • Graph neural network with multi-head attention and a contrastive (InfoNCE) objective.
  • Benchmarked against CopyKAT, scMalignantFinder, Cancer-Finder, and PreCanCell, consistently outperforming them.
  • Extended to 10x Visium spatial transcriptomics, recovering tumor regions that match histopathology annotations.
  • Robust in heterogeneous tumor microenvironments such as pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC).

OncoSpot Submitted, Genome Research

Lead author

A clonal simulator that generates spatial transcriptomics data with known copy-number clones, giving ground truth for benchmarking cancer spatial methods across tumor evolutionary modes. Open source, with a parameter-estimation workflow that calibrates simulations to your own reference data. Code on GitHub.

scLongTree Accepted, Briefings in Bioinformatics

Co-first author

A tool for inferring longitudinal tumor phylogenies from single-cell DNA sequencing data. I am co-first author and carried out the journal revision, including benchmarking against RobustClone and SCG.
